# Artwork sources

All five illustrations were generated on OpenArt with Seedream 4.5, 9:16 at
2K (1600x2848), 15 credits each, on 2026-09-05. `scripts/fetch-art.sh`
downloads them into this folder under the names below. Until it has been run,
the files here are procedural stand-ins from `scripts/make-placeholders.py`.

Shared style prefix for every prompt: "Vertical storybook illustration in a
detailed engraved ink and gouache style with fine hatched linework and muted
painterly colour." Every prompt ends "No text, no lettering, no watermark, no
border."
